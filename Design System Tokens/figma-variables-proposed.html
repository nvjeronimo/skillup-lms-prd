Brand and scheme are two independent mode axes: a frame picks Brand = SkillUp and Semantic = Light separately. The brand-* semantic tokens alias the Brand collection, so switching a brand mode reskins everything. A new brand is one more mode here (Figma Pro is limited to 10 modes). Each mode exports to a brand JSON for the [data-brand] CSS. No hex in Semantic or Brand — only aliases.
