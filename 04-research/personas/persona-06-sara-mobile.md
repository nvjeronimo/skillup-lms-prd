# P06 · Sara Lindqvist — "The Mobile Micro-Learner"

> Does almost everything on her phone in short bursts — commute, lunch, couch. Even joins live sessions from mobile. The persona that breaks desktop-only assumptions and validates the 375w responsive screens.

## Profile
- Role: Product analyst, always moving between meetings.
- Context: Rarely at a desk for learning; phone is the device.
- Time available: ~3–4h/week in 10–15 min micro-sessions.
- Schedule: Opportunistic — commute and breaks.

## Behavior & motivation
- Goal: Keep up with Product Analytics without carving out desk time.
- Drivers: Convenience, bite-size progress, time-respecting notifications.
- Frictions: Long topics don't fit a 12-min window; live hard to attend on mobile; small tap targets.
- Engagement: Steady but shallow — many short, easily-interrupted sessions. Her data validates mobile density and resume.

## Temperament & tech literacy
- **Temperament:** Pragmatic, adaptive, interrupt-driven multitasker. Time-boxed, optimizes for "good enough now", resumes constantly.
- **Tech literacy — Split (mobile 5/5, desktop 3/5):** Highly fluent with mobile gestures and patterns; less comfortable with desktop conventions. Expects mobile-grade ergonomics everywhere.
- **Testing implications:** Stresses touch ergonomics, gesture expectations carried onto desktop, cross-device resumability, and parity between mobile and desktop affordances.
