# P05 · Leo Martins — "The Impatient Explorer"

> High intent, blocked execution. Enrolled in a gated program and constantly runs into locked courses, pre-work prerequisites, and VILT sessions that aren't open yet. The persona that stress-tests every locked/blocked/gated state.

## Profile
- Role: Content marketer, fast and eager.
- Context: Wants to skip to the parts he cares about; the structure won't let him.
- Time available: ~7h/week, would do more if unlocked.
- Schedule: Tries to binge; gating forces waiting.

## Behavior & motivation
- Goal: Reach the SEO and paid-media modules he actually wants.
- Drivers: Curiosity, impatience, skip-to-the-good-parts.
- Frictions: Locked courses, pre-work gates, VILT not yet open. Frustrated by "why can't I start this?".
- Engagement: High intent, blocked flow. His data is the gating/prerequisite testbed.

## Temperament & tech literacy
- **Temperament:** Impulsive, impatient, low frustration tolerance. Acts before reading, hates waits and locks, pushes every button to see what happens.
- **Tech literacy — Capable but reckless (4/5):** Technically able but skips onboarding, ignores instructions, and clicks fast. Surfaces edge cases through misuse, not inability.
- **Testing implications:** Stresses blocked/error-state messaging clarity, guardrails, rapid/disallowed click handling, and whether gating is explained *before* the click. The persona most likely to break a flow.
