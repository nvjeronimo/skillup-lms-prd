> Product decision locked during discovery. Push back NOW if you disagree — mid-build reversal is 10x more expensive.

**Decision** — Mentoring is unlimited 1:1 asynchronous messaging, not scheduled/booked sessions and not group threads. One mentor assigned per learner at enrollment.

**Why**
- "Lower friction. Scales better. Learners actually use it."
- Async messaging removes the calendar-coordination barrier that suppresses booked-session usage.

**Source** — Figma `3832-18102` "Key Decisions" + BA business rules, 2026-06-08. [`../LMS-HANDOFF/BA/03-business-rules.md`](../LMS-HANDOFF/BA/03-business-rules.md) (BR-18, BR-19, BR-20).

**edX basis** — n/a.

**Design** — Mentor messaging UI is Phase 2 (out of Phase 1 build scope); node-id unknown.

**Dev impact** — BR-18 (scope), BR-19 (SLA: "Typically responds within 1 day"), BR-20 (fair-use nudge > 10 unanswered messages). Note: mentor SLA is also an open product question (see INDEX).

**Alternatives rejected** — Booked/scheduled sessions and group threads: rejected — higher friction, worse scaling, lower actual usage.
