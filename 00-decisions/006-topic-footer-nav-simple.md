> Product decision locked during discovery. Push back NOW if you disagree — mid-build reversal is 10x more expensive.

**Decision** — The Topic Footer Nav is Previous · Unit info · Next only — no context-aware action chips.

**Why**
- "We tried context-aware action chips. Reverted. Don't reopen — locked decision."
- The footer's one job is linear traversal; overloading it with contextual actions muddied that.
