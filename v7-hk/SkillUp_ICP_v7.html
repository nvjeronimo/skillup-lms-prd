Welcome back. In this unit we look at the product development lifecycle through the lens of AI-augmented workflows.
0:18
Understanding this lifecycle is critical — it's the backbone of everything a product manager does, from discovery to launch.
0:38
The lifecycle begins long before any code is written — with deep understanding of customer needs and unmet pain points.
0:58
Traditionally, this research phase was time-consuming — hours of interviews, synthesis workshops, affinity mapping.
1:14
This is exactly where AI tools can dramatically compress the research phase — turning 50 interviews into structured themes in minutes.
1:34
From there you move into ideation — generating and stress-testing hypotheses about what the product should do and for whom.
1:54
ChatGPT is particularly powerful here. Give it your customer persona and generate 20 product concepts in 60 seconds.
2:14
Prototyping follows ideation. MVP development should be ruthless — test your riskiest assumption first, not the easiest to build.
2:34
Once you have a prototype, move to testing. Dogfooding, then external beta users. Gather signal, not just feedback.
2:54
Finally, rollout. Remember — rollout is the beginning of a new feedback loop that feeds straight back into discovery.
3:20
Next we look at user segmentation — and how AI can identify segments you didn't even know existed. See you there.
