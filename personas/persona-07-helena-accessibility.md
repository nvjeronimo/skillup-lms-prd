# P07 · Helena Costa — "The Accessibility-First Senior"

> 63, changing careers after decades in a non-digital role. Uses screen magnification, larger text, and keyboard navigation. Low digital confidence. The persona that decides whether the product is actually usable by everyone — not just the median user.

## Profile
- Role: Former operations manager re-skilling into a people/team role.
- Context: Self-funded, motivated but unsure she belongs in an online program.
- Time available: ~3h/week, daytime, quiet environment.
- Schedule: Fixed weekday mornings; dislikes surprises and time pressure.

## Behavior & motivation
- Goal: Earn a credential that proves she can lead in a modern, digital-first team.
- Drivers: Proving capability to herself and to employers, staying relevant.
- Frictions: Small text and targets, fast interactions, unlabeled icons, uncaptioned video, anything that assumes speed or prior app knowledge.
- Engagement: Persistent but fragile — one bad blocked/confusing moment and she stops for days.

## Temperament & tech literacy
- **Temperament:** Patient and determined, but easily discouraged. Will retry, then give up quietly if the interface fights her.
- **Tech literacy — Low (1/5):** Limited experience with web apps. Doesn't recognize common patterns (hamburger menus, icon buttons, drag). Reads instructions literally.
- **Accessibility needs (real, not simulated):**
  - **Low vision** — uses OS magnification + browser zoom (150–200%), prefers large text, high contrast; relies on a screen reader for dense passages.
  - **Reduced fine motor control** — small or close-together tap/click targets are hard; needs generous hit areas and keyboard-operable controls; struggles with drag/hover-only actions.
  - **Hearing loss** — needs captions/transcripts for all VILT and recordings; audio-only content excludes her.
- **Testing implications:** The product's accessibility conscience. Stresses WCAG basics — contrast, text scaling/reflow at 200%, focus order, keyboard-only operation, target size (44px+), visible focus, labels/alt text, captions/transcripts, and reduced-motion. If a flow works for Helena, it works for everyone.
