# P03 · Dev Okafor — "The Stall-and-Returner"

> Enrolls with intent, then work swallows him for two weeks. Several items are behind, one is effectively abandoned, one was finished long ago, and he just reopened the app. The at-risk / re-engagement profile.

## Profile
- Role: Junior data analyst with heavy sprint workload.
- Context: Self-enrolled, no manager pressure — easy to deprioritize.
- Time available: 2–3h/week, erratic; often skips a week.
- Schedule: None reliable; studies in bursts.

## Behavior & motivation
- Goal: Get Python-fluent for analytics, stop leaning on the senior analyst.
- Drivers: Practical skill over credentials; wants quick wins.
- Frictions: Falls behind, then feels too far gone to return. Missed a live session, unaware it was recorded.
- Engagement: Declining but salvageable. His data is the catch-up / churn-risk testbed.

## Temperament & tech literacy
- **Temperament:** Distractible, avoidant, low conscientiousness. Easily pulled away, procrastinates, and dodges the things he's behind on. Skilled but unmotivated.
- **Tech literacy — High but disengaged (4/5):** Fast with keyboard, multiple tabs, gets around quickly. Friction is attention, not ability. Skims screens, ignores soft nudges.
- **Testing implications:** Stresses notifications and re-engagement that must cut through, resumability after long context loss, and frictionless catch-up. His failures are motivational, not usability — useful to separate the two.
